        About this guide

        Missable rule: a trophy counts as missable only when its official description implies a one-chance condition — undetected, back-to-back, a single use of something, a story choice, or a second attempt that counts. Chapter and mission trophies are never flagged missable: they unlock by playing the campaign.

        Hidden trophies: the hidden flag follows the official PSN list (28 hidden). Spoiler mode above hides story trophies until you earn them, whatever their hidden flag — except the platinum, which is always shown because it is the goal of this guide.

        An independent, fan-made guide. Not affiliated with the game's publisher or the platform holder. No official logo or artwork is used here, and this page loads nothing over the network — every file it needs sits in its own folder.
